Wordmark

The name, set in Inter 600, lowercase, with the dot in the accent colour. There is no symbol.

aave.watch
  • Letter-spacing −0.02em. Minimum size 16px.
  • Only the dot takes colour. The letters are the text colour of the theme.
  • No Aave logo, ghost mark or GHO mark anywhere in the product.

  • Text on any colour fill is on-fill. Light text on the accent fails contrast (2.8 to 1).
  • All text pairs meet 4.5 to 1 on bg, surface and surface-2, in both themes.

  • Protocol terms keep the protocol’s spelling: Hub, Spoke, reserve, drawn, premium, deficit.
  • A state number is never shown without its block.
  • Errors say what failed, what the reader is looking at instead, and the one thing to do.
  • Empty states say what is absent and where, not that the screen is empty.
  • No exclamation marks, no emoji, no “please”.
  • Sample data is always labelled as sample data.
