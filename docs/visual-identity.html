Aave, live.

The friend who watches Aave for you: warm, familiar, a little funny, and exact. Every figure on this page is sample data.

Logo

The Tick is a W for "watch" drawn as a price line. The name is one lowercase word in Onest 700. The dot at the end of the line pulses only while the page is receiving live data.

Colour

One meaning per colour. Pink is ours. Four colours name a kind of asset, three name a state, and nothing else is coloured: actions, roles and chains are neutral.

Links, buttons and switches

The site is read-only, so there is one quiet button and no primary one. Every token, pool, market and address is a link to its page. Focus is a 2px ring on everything you can reach with the keyboard: press Tab here to see it.

How a page opens

Now, Rates and Close calls open straight on the stat strip, with no headline above it. The line under a stat is plain text; a pill appears only for a change or a state. A wallet page opens on its gauge, shown further down.

Chart

Minimal and exact: the value and its change above, one thin line with a soft fill, a dotted grid, round ticks on the right, time labels on real boundaries. Point at it, drag along it, or use the arrow keys.

Tables, rows and bars

Names left, numbers right, at most six columns. A column header sorts the table when pressed, with an arrow on the sorted column. On a phone a table keeps the name and its two most useful numbers. "Lent out" and "limit used" are two different bars and are named.
